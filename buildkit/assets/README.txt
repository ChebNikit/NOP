Optional author assets (kept out of the kit on purpose).
If you want the "Support the author" images inside NOP, put avatar.jpg and the QR images here
(donate_crypto_qr.jpg, donate_ozon_qr.jpg, donate_boosty_qr.jpg). Everything in this folder is copied
to <install folder>\assets by the build. An empty folder is fine: NOP then simply shows no images.
